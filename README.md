## Selected Work

- 🏆 **[Ant LingBot Embodied AI Challenge](https://github.com/AlexDrives/lingbot-competiton-fbfyfbf)** — Post-trained **LingBot-VLA 2.0 (6B)** across 50 bimanual tasks in RoboTwin 2.0 (**6th / 1,855** in prelims, `82.2%` clean) and co-built our **48-hour real-robot dual-arm system** on AgileX Cobot Magic (**Finals Excellence Award**): real-robot teleop & DAgger pipelines (`646` demos), async **Real-Time Chunking (RTC)** inference, and a DeepSeek **System-2 subtask harness** for long-horizon table cleanup.
- 🧩 **[Real2Sim & Port–Mate Scene Reasoning @ ABB Robotics](https://portfolio-rouge-three-eznvbawdug.vercel.app/#experience/abb)** — Translating visual observations and language instructions into physics-ready simulation assets and composable geometric Mate constraints (`on`, `seat`, `insert`, `free_in`, `joint`), verified under gravity and contact in **OmniGibson**.
- 🧬 **[RNA-GCP-VQVAE](https://github.com/AlexDrives/RNA-GCP-VQVAE)** — Extended geometric vector-quantized autoencoders from proteins to **RNA 3D structure tokenization & reconstruction** with homology-controlled `RNAsolo / cd-hit-est → HDF5` pipelines, 6-angle RNA backbone dihedrals, and an **AlphaFold2-style IPA decoder** under trajectory-level **FAPE** supervision (`1.08 Å` MAE · `0.79` TM-score).
- 🎲 **[Autonomous Robotic Dice Stacking](https://github.com/AlexDrives/Dice-detection-and-auto-stacking-with-OpenMV-and-OpenCV)** — Closed-loop camera undistortion, dense 27-dice single-frame perception, homography + quadratic-residual calibration (`450+` pairs, `0.740 mm` mean error), and 4-DOF suction-arm control (**6 dice stacked in 26s · #1 / 38** at SJTU).
- ♠️ **[Kuhn Poker: Self-Play RL vs. CFR](https://github.com/AlexDrives/kuhn_poker-exp)** — Diagnosed policy oscillation and exploitability collapse in naive self-play REINFORCE against **CFR / CFR+** Nash convergence in **OpenSpiel**.
